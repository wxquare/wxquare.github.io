ROMEO:
Good morrow to you both. What counterfeit did I give you?

BENVOLIO:
The salutation by my faith, the counterfeit money.

ROMEO:
Thou hast most kindly hit it.

MERCUTIO:
Come between us, good Benvolio; my wits faint.

BENVOLIO:
I pray thee, good Mercutio, let us retire.

MERCUTIO:
Come, we burn daylight, ho!

ROMEO:
Nay, that's not so.

MERCUTIO:
I mean, sir, in delay we waste our lights in vain,
like lamps by day. Take our good meaning, for our judgment
sits five times in that ere once in our five wits.

ROMEO:
And we mean well in going to this masque;
but 'tis no wit to go.

BENVOLIO:
Why, 'tis no care; we shall not be there.

ROMEO:
I dream'd a dream to-night.

MERCUTIO:
And so did I.

ROMEO:
Well, what was yours?

MERCUTIO:
That dreamers often lie.
